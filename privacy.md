# Privacy Policy

Middle-Earth Maps by itself doesn't collect any data. It's **fully offline**.

However, two official versions of Middle-Earth Maps can be accessed through online websites, and thus usual cookies and online activity tracking data will be obtained by your requests to these sites.

## Online Use

- Official Website (https://lotr.masterpose.dev): [Master Pose privacy policy](https://masterpose.dev/en/privacy) will apply.
- Alt Itch.io App (https://masterpose.itch.io/middle-earth-maps): [Itch.io privacy policy](https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy) will apply.

## Android Use

As previously stated, the app is **fully offline**, and thus, no internet permission is ever required.
